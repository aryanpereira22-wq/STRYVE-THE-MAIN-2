# Project Context: Gamified Fitness iOS App with Map & Ranking System

## Goal
Build a gamified fitness iOS application that uses geolocation, a ranking system, and social features to engage users in competitive and fun fitness challenges. The app should be scalable, visually appealing, and addictive enough to retain users through social competition and visible progression.

## Core Concept
This iOS app will use the user's real-world activity (steps, running distance, cycling, etc.) and place them on a **map-based leaderboard** where users can:
- Compete with others in their local area or globally
- Earn ranks, badges, and achievements
- See their position visually on a map
- Participate in seasonal events, team battles, and challenge zones

## Key Features (to be expanded later)
- **Live map-based fitness zones**
- **Real-time ranking system (local & global)**
- **Activity tracking integration (Apple HealthKit / CoreMotion)**
- **User profiles & avatars**
- **XP/level system with rewards**
- **Challenge creation/joining (solo or team)**
- **Social features (add friends, comment, challenge others)**

## Target Audience
- Fitness enthusiasts
- Gamers who enjoy competitive mechanics
- Casual users looking for fitness motivation
- Students and office workers looking to make walking/running fun

## Monetization Ideas
- In-app purchases for avatars or boosts
- Premium subscription (extra analytics, exclusive challenges)
- Brand-sponsored challenges and rewards
- Ads (optional; ideally minimal or opt-in)

## Tech Stack Preferences
- Platform: iOS (Swift / SwiftUI)
- Location: CoreLocation / MapKit or Mapbox
- Health Tracking: HealthKit
- Backend: Firebase / Supabase or custom Node.js
- Real-time Rankings: WebSockets or Firebase Realtime DB
- Design: Clean, minimalist with gamified UI elements (badges, ranks, progress bars)

## Competitive Edge
Unlike standard fitness apps, this app integrates real-time maps and competitive rankings to foster motivation through gameplay. Think "Strava meets Clash Royale"—users gain XP, ranks, and local dominance.
